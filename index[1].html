DIAGNOSTIC CHECK · COURSE ENTRY

Prior Knowledge Check

The Moodle course includes an initial knowledge check (0.5 Előzetes tudásmérő). This illustrative activity demonstrates how prior understanding can be surfaced before a lesson.

Learning prompt

Which statement best describes the purpose of regenerative grazing?

A fixed, continuous approach may overlook recovery needs.
Correct. Adaptive management considers timing, intensity and recovery.
Grazing exclusion is not automatically the right solution for all grasslands.

Source note: Standalone, English-language educational adaptation inspired by the named activity in the Moodle backup. It is not the original H5P source or a verified learner outcome.

OBSERVATION · MODULE 2

Reading the Grassland

Inspired by the Moodle activities about signs of grassland condition (2.4.1) and management needs (2.1.0/a and 2.3.1).

Learning prompt

Which observation most clearly suggests healthy vegetation cover?

Exposed soil suggests possible vulnerability to erosion.
Correct. Ground cover and diverse vegetation are useful signals to examine.
Bare soil and runoff may indicate deterioration.

BRANCHING SCENARIO · MODULE 3

One Parcel, One Decision

A short English-language adaptation of the parcel-management decision exercise (3.5).

Learning prompt

One week after grazing, the vegetation remains low, trampling is visible and rainfall has been limited. What would you do next?

Returning too early may weaken recovery. Recheck regrowth and soil condition first.
A reasonable decision: monitor recovery and postpone regrazing while conditions remain poor.
Additional grazing pressure may amplify damage under these conditions.

FORMATIVE ASSESSMENT · MODULE 3

Practical Knowledge Check

An illustrative knowledge check reflecting the practical-principles module and its 3.6 self-check.

Learning prompt

What is an appropriate basis for deciding when livestock should revisit a parcel?

A calendar can guide planning, but site conditions must inform decisions.
Correct. Observation and context improve grazing decisions.
Logistics matter, but should not override ecological assessment.
